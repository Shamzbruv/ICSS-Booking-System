WHAT IT'S ACTUALLY LIKE

A day with ICSS Bookings.

Here's how a normal day goes for a Kingston barbershop running on ICSS Bookings.

  1. 7:00 am
    Your day, sorted

    Your phone shows today's agenda: 6 appointments, first at 9:00, 1 payment to review.

  2. 8:15 am
    A deposit comes in by Lynk

    Kemar booked last night and uploaded his Lynk screenshot. You tap Approve — confirmed, invoice sent.

  3. 8:30 am
    A heads-up before every client

    “Up next at 9:00: Andre — Skin fade.” No more checking the book.

  4. 12:40 pm
    A client reschedules themselves

    Sasha's shift changed. She picks Thursday at 2:00 from her email link. Your calendar updates, and you didn't have to reply to a single message.

  5. 3:00 pm
    A walk-in calls

    You book them in from the dashboard in 20 seconds. They get the same confirmation and invoice as everyone else.

  6. 6:30 pm
    Reviews roll in

    Today's clients are invited to review you. Five stars appear on your website by dinner.

  7. 11:20 pm
    An after-hours request

    A client asks for 7:00 am Saturday, outside your normal hours, with your after-hours fee. You'll decide in the morning.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions? Let’s clear them up.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Choose a theme, add your services and use the dashboard to manage your page and appointments.

Can my clients book from their phone?

Yes. Your booking page works on phones, tablets and desktop browsers, so clients can book wherever they find your link.

Can clients pay a deposit when they book?

Yes. For each service you choose no payment, a deposit (fixed or percentage) or full payment. Clients can pay through Lynk, Fygaro, EasyPay, First Atlantic Commerce / PowerTranz, PayPal or bank transfer — whichever you switch on.

Do my clients get an invoice?

Yes. Every booking gets a branded PDF invoice with your logo, the service, the appointment, payments received and the balance due. It's attached to the confirmation email, and you can download it any time from your dashboard.

What if a client wants a time outside my hours?

Turn on after-hours requests and clients can ask for a time outside your normal schedule. You can add an after-hours fee, and nothing is booked until you approve it.

Can clients reschedule or cancel themselves?

Yes. Every confirmation email has a “Manage my booking” link. You decide the rules: how close to the appointment changes stop, how many reschedules are allowed, and whether you approve each new time. Clients always give a reason.

Is it really made in Jamaica?

Yes. ICSS Bookings is designed, built and supported in Jamaica by iCreate Solutions & Services — with Jamaican payment options, JMD pricing and local support built in.

What happens after the free trial?

Your subscription continues at US$31.67 per month under the billing terms you accept at checkout. Cancel before renewal if you don’t want to continue.
