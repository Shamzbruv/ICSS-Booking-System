Your choice at booking

By checking the WhatsApp box and submitting your booking, you accept these terms and ask ICSS Bookings to send updates about that booking to the WhatsApp number you entered. For a phone booking, the business may record your permission after sharing these terms with you. Use a number you control and check that it is correct.

You can leave the box unchecked and still book. Booking emails continue when an email address is provided. These terms supplement the ICSS Bookings Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Messages you may receive

ICSS Bookings may send a request acknowledgement, confirmation, appointment reminders, rescheduling updates and cancellation notices. The number of messages depends on the booking, any changes and the business’s reminder settings. Your permission covers these booking messages. WhatsApp uses your internet connection, so your usual data charges may apply.

Delivery can be delayed or unavailable. Check your booking email or contact the business if you need to confirm the status of your appointment.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to the number that sent your ICSS Bookings WhatsApp message. This stops future ICSS Bookings WhatsApp updates and team alerts to that number. Your booking and email updates continue. You can also contact ICSS support for help with an opt-out.

Data used for delivery

ICSS Bookings uses Twilio and WhatsApp (Meta) to send these messages. They process the recipient’s number and message content, which can include names, the booked business and service, appointment times and the booking reference. ICSS Bookings records consent, opt-outs and delivery information to manage messaging and troubleshoot delivery.

See the ICSS Bookings Privacy Policy, Twilio Privacy Notice and WhatsApp Privacy Policy for details.

Business team alerts

Businesses can configure team alerts for booking activity and reminders. These alerts may include the customer’s name and booking details. Business admins must share these terms, obtain each recipient’s permission, verify that the recipient is authorized to see the business’s bookings, and remove recipients who withdraw permission or no longer need access.

Booking and messaging help

Contact the business directly for appointment changes, payments or refunds. For WhatsApp delivery, consent or privacy questions, email icreatesolutions.ja@gmail.com.
