How it works for car wash & detailing

Set up your service packages

List exterior washes, interior cleaning and full details with the time you expect each one to take. Explain what is included before the client books.

Collect useful vehicle information

Ask for the vehicle make and model and any areas that need special attention. For mobile work, ask for the service address in the booking notes.

Manage travel and turnaround

Use buffers between appointments and block unavailable times. Decide whether longer details require an upfront booking deposit.

Questions before you get started

Can I use it for mobile detailing?

Yes. The detailing form includes a place for the client to share an address or describe what needs attention. Confirm travel coverage and any extra charges with the client.
