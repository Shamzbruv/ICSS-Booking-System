How it works for car rental

Collect the details before the call

Ask for the rental dates and vehicle preference when the customer submits an enquiry. For example, a customer landing at Norman Manley or Sangster can tell you the airport they want to collect from.

Set hours for rental enquiries

Publish the times your team can take calls or discuss bookings. You can also record phone enquiries from your dashboard when a customer calls you directly.

Handle deposits with clear terms

If your business charges for a consultation or appointment, set the payment requirement for that service. Confirm the vehicle, rental price and any separate rental deposit with the customer before handover.

Questions before you get started

Does an enquiry reserve a car for the entire rental period?

The customer books a consultation time and submits their preferred dates. Your company checks its fleet and confirms vehicle availability, documents, insurance and the rental agreement separately.

Can customers tell us where they want to pick up the car?

Yes. The rental form asks for their vehicle preference and pickup location, alongside their requested start and end dates.

Can we show vehicle options on our website?

Yes. Use your booking website to describe the vehicle classes you offer and add photos. Keep your fleet calendar as the source for vehicle availability.
