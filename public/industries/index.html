How to choose the best booking system for your business

Start with a booking you handled this week. Use the public demos to check the booking form, then use a trial account to test deposits, confirmations and changes.

1. Match the booking to the job

Clinics and tutors can offer set appointment times. A cleaner may need the address and job size first, while an event supplier may need to check the date and equipment before quoting. Check what customers need to enter and when your team can approve the request.

2. Try taking a deposit in JMD

Check which payment methods your customers can use, which need a receipt review and when the booking is confirmed. In ICSS Bookings, you can set a fixed or percentage deposit for a service and choose which payment methods to enable.

3. Book from a customer’s phone

Open the booking link from WhatsApp on your phone. Check that you can see the service, price, policy and available times before entering your details. Use a test booking on your trial site to see the confirmation email and invoice.

4. Test a change to the schedule

Block a time you already have booked, then try moving a booking as a customer. Check when customers can reschedule or cancel, when you need to approve a change and what the customer sees when a time is unavailable.

5. Check how much of the website you can edit

Decide whether you need a booking link or a full site with your services, gallery and contact details. Open the website builder playground to see how much you can change before signing up.

6. Work out the total monthly cost

Check the subscription currency, the payment provider’s fees and what is included in the plan. Compare the cost for the services you actually offer and check how to reach support if you need help setting up.

If your industry is outside these categories, try the general business template with your own services and booking policies.
