The Problem

Running a shop without an appointment system is leaving money on the table

Auto shops in Jamaica face a constant tension between walk-ins and booked customers. Structure your workflow and serve more clients better.

🚗 Unpredictable walk-in flow

When everyone shows up without an appointment, your bay backs up unpredictably and quality of work suffers under time pressure.

Customers calling to ask if their vehicle is ready takes your mechanic away from the actual work. Systematic confirmation eliminates this.
